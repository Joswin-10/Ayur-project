Stirin gives clear instructions to pupils where certain
words are likely to confuse them. In the text, godhitmah
sumanah samau he notes that the word sumanah (wheat) is one
ending in a (akdranto ’yam, p. 564). This is helpful as sumanas
also means a god (p. 7), a flower (p. 225), a variety of jasmine
(p. 258) all of which are words ending in s or sakdranta and
Siirin indicates them as such in their proper places, sdnto ’yam
(p. 258), santah striyam (p. 226). Similarly in the text sitrani
nart tantavah, the word nart may sound somewhat puzzling to
young students and hence he gives a clear explanation of the
word, ` `
