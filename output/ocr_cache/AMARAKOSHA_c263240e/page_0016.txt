and 4 respectively, are to be found ‘ yasya jiidnadayasindhoh ’, the
very beginning of the Ak., and ‘ samahrtyanyatantrani’ (v. 2).

the literature connected with the Ak. forms a literature by
itself (See New Catalogus Catalogorum, vol. 1 (rev.), pp. 318-32).
