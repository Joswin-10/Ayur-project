INTRODUCTION ११118

The Nanartha-(homonymous)varga is the biggest section
in Ak.; still the author says that only some homonymous
words have been dealt with in this section, beginning with
words that end in ka (naka) and ending with those that end in
ha (paribharha).

नानार्थाः केऽपि कान्तादिककरष्वेवात्र कीर्तिताः |

Siirin says at the very outset of this Varga that he intends to
include meanings left out by the author due to his fear of
increasing the balk of the work:

अनुक्ता ग्रन्थकारेण म्रन्थगीरवभीरणा | (४. 1)

The inclusion of additional meanings necessarily obliges him
to recast the original verses of Amara, retaining, as far as.
possible, Amara’s own words. These alterations and addi-
tions have been noted from time to time in various lexicons.
after Amara, such as the Vaijayanti, Visvaprakasa and Abhidhana-
ratnamala. Sirin says that he consulted such works as were
available to him:

qe नानाथेषदस्याथां यथामति !

His explanations are in prose in between the verses, e.g..
for akdse tridive ndkah, Strin has the version

अम्बरे सुरखोके च नाकशब्दः प्रकीर्तितः |
explained as
न विद्यतेऽकं दुःखमत्रेति नाकः |

The word ksvedé (poison, etc.) is dismissed by Amara with
ksveda vamsabalakapi, no doubt referring to his ksveda tu simha-
nadah syat in the Ksatriyavarga (p. 546). Sirin elaborates it
by giving more than ten meanings:

कणैरोगे विषे eat gee कुरिठे ati |
