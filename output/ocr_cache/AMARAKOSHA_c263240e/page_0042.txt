Amara has set forth in the beginning of his work certain
devices for indicating the genders of words, etc. whose signi-
ficance is explained with suitable illustrations by Sarin. The
first device is that the distinction of gender is made by the form.
of the word such as the endings in a; 4, i or a; and am for
mas., fem. and neuter gender respectively.
