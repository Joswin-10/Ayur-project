As a rule Amara gives words in the singular number
only. However, godhe and tale are in the dual as these gloves
are always used in a pair.

The words madhu and mdadhvika (wine) are mentioned by
Amara as neuter (advayoh), but as Durgasimha states they may
be used both in the mas. and neuter due to their inclusion
in the Ardharcadi-gana. Malli. says that the word advayoh
is reminiscent of its optional character.
