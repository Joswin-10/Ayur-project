Where the text of Amara is ambiguous or seemingly
incomplete Sarin takes care to see that the student has a
clear grasp of the situation. In

विश्याचाः सदैकस्वे सर्वा; संस्येयसंख्ययोः
the numerals 20 and above are treated as singular in number
and used both with reference to samkhyeya (objects to be
numbered) and samkhya (number) as vimsatih patah and vimsatih
patiném. Surin draws our attention to the fact that this is

true not merely for numbers 20 and above, but the number 19 |
too must be added to the list.
