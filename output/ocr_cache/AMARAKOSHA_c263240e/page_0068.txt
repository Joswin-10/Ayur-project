The words indindira and other synonyms of madhukara (bee)
given by the author of Sesa are defya words according to Svamin
and Malli. accepts them as such:

इन्दिन्दिरचश्चरीकरोरम्बभससख्शब्दा देश्या इति स्वामी | (p. 352)

Malli. gives the Telugu meaning of Amara’s words almost
everywhere in his comm. Some of the Telugu equivalents
may be archaic and sound strange to modern ears. प्र
gives vepa for the Nimba tree while Malli. has vempa (p. 252).
Khadira is candracetiu to Stirin and candracettu to Malli.
Siirin gives the word devatédi for which Malli. supplies dapara-
dangi. Jasmine is molla to Sarin and mulla to Malli. (p. 257).
The medicinal plant bald is cittémudapucettu in Sirin’s comm.
whereas muttuvapulagamu is given in Malli.’s comm. (p. 278).
Kokila (cuckoo) is koyala (Koyila Bi, Ks) in Siirin’s work but
Kokila in Malli.’s.
