Another feature in the Nanartha section, helpful to
students, is the addition of the meanings of the words in fem.
gender which have a large number of senses unconnected
with the word in the mas. The word harini is dealt with by
the side of harinah bringing out the additional meanings pos-
sessed by the former (v. 107) such as the name of a goddess.
2. metre, a golden statue, etc.
