In the same context the word nighnah (dependent) is explained
by Bhanuji as
निहन्यते fect | हन्तेः घञर्थं कः ।
whereas Stirin says:
[निहन्यते] नियम्यते sao तिरक्ियत इति fa: |
Svamin has: | ।
निहन्यते निघः | Tae कविधानम्‌ (12. 239). |
Sarvananda’s explanation too is meagre in most places, though
the grammatical derivation is full. For nighnak he has
निहन्यत इति fa: । aay कः।
and for dyattah he has yati prayatne, ktah (IV, pp. 7-8).1

(IIT, ए. 139);
and by Bhanuji as ‘ ajanam. “‘ aja gatau”?’. He then gives a

number of grammatical rules to show the correctness of the
